📝 وصف المنتج

Practice your learning at home. Build skills, make progress and grow confidence. This book can help your child by providing a whole year of ready to go activities and support on key maths topics. Did you know that children in Year 5 need to know and use prime numbers, prime factors and composite numbers; know all tables facts up to 12 × 12; understand and use equivalences between metric and imperial units such as inches, pounds and pints? This full colour workbook contains 128 pages of engaging activities to help consolidate your child's learning and boost their confidence, allowing them to feel prepared, capable and ready to tackle new challenges at school. The content is divided into National Curriculum topics (for example, Addition and subtraction, Fractions, Geometry and so on). This book offers essential practice for home or in the classroom. This workbook for home learning is aligned with the National Curriculum and helps reinforce topics taught in school, giving children extra opportunities to practise and succeed. A linked website provides additional activities and answers. Includes helpful 'quick tips' at the top of most pages for parents to support learning. Quickly builds confidence through regular, achievable activities that encourage independent learning. Fun practice activities and questions to help children check their understanding. Includes a progress chart to tick off as each chapter is completed and a certificate to motivate learning. This series is ideal for: Children aged 4-11 (Reception - Year 6) Families looking for structured practice that fits around daily life Parents who want to support learning linked directly to topics their children will be taught in school Children who benefit from additional or repeated practice Packed with colourful illustrations, engaging activities and confidence-boosting practice, these workbooks help children stay on track with their learning throughout the school year. Available for English and Maths. For more targeted practice in Addition & Subtraction, Algebra, Fractions, Times Tables, Mental Maths, and Multiplication & Division. Try Scholastic's Maths Skills workbooks to help keep children motivated, engaged and support them as they achieve mastery. Or try Scholastic's Made Simple maths workbooks for children who need further revision and practice. With clear explanations and helpful hints to support independent learning these workbooks refresh key skills from the previous school year before introducing new challenges.
