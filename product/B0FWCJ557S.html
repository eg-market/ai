The Essager 2-in-1 USB A to USB-C + iPhone Compatible Cable offers the convenience of two connectors in one durable cable. With a USB-C plug on one end and interchangeable iPhone-Compatible Port and USB-C tips on the other, it charges both iOS and Android devices with ease. Supporting up to 65W of Power Delivery on the USB-C side, the cable provides fast charging for laptops and smartphones, while the iPhone-Compatible Port ensures efficient charging for iPhones and iPads. It also supports data sync across both connectors. Braided nylon construction resists frays, while reinforced ends ensure lasting performance. Compact and travel-ready, this cable eliminates the need for multiple chargers.
